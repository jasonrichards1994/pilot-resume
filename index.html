Risk Management for Business Aviation

Corporate flying requires balancing mission completion with safety. My approach:

Pre-Flight Decision Making

  • Thorough weather analysis including destination alternates and enroute conditions
  • NOTAM review with attention to TFRs, airspace changes, and airport restrictions
  • Weight & balance with realistic passenger/baggage assumptions
  • Performance calculations for actual conditions, not book numbers
  • Backup plans established before departure

Single-Pilot Resource Management

  • Automation management: know when to use it and when to hand-fly
  • Workload management during high-density operations
  • Situational awareness through continuous cross-checking
  • No hesitation to request ATC assistance or delay

Decision Points

  • Establish weather minimums for each flight based on conditions
  • Know when to divert rather than continue to deteriorating destination
  • Quick to return or delay rather than accept unacceptable risk
  • Clear communication with passengers about delays or diversions
